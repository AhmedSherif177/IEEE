#Telecom Customer Churn Dashboard


A dashboard visualizing important information from a telecom dataset using Power Bi. We create new measure called churn_rate which calaculates the percentage of churn customers among total cusomers

1- 5 cards showing total number of customers , number of customers who churned and their percentage, total revenue and average monthly charge.
2- A pie chart of customers' gender. It shows that 63% percent of customers are female, which should be considered when planning the next marketing campaign.
3- A 100% percent stacked column chart showing different contracts and percentages of customer status. It shows that most customers who churn have Month to Month contracts.
4- A column chart of churn rate by internet type. It shows that most churn customers have fiper optic internet.
5-A column chart of top churn reasons. And the top reasons are related to competitors having better devices and making better offers.
6-A drill down bar chart of top categories for churn and top category is the competitor.
7-A column chart of churn by tenure. It shows that most churn happen between 6-12 months of tenure, so maybe it would be good to offer customers who stay over 6 months a one year contract at a discount.
