#Movie_dataset_visualization

A program to visualize important information from a movies datasetusing pandas, matplotlip and seaborn. First we read the data and take a look at it to make sure the data is cleaned. Then we see the visualizations:

1- A bar plot to visualize popularity by genres
2- A bar plot to visualize top 15 popular movies in the dataset and their genres
3- A bar plot to visualize least 15 popular movies in the dataset and their genres
4- A histogram to visualize number of movie releases through the years
5- A barplot to visualize top 18 rated movies with more than a 1000 rating in the dataset with their genres
6- A heatmap to visualize correlation between numeric columns
